Algebraic topology journals


One key to successfully publishing a research article is to submit your work to an editor whose mathematical interests are close to the topic of your submission. This page lists the names of journals whose editorial board includes at least one algebraic topologist. I have found this list to be very useful in deciding where to submit an article, and maybe others will find it helpful as well. Please do not interpret this page as an endorsement or recommendation of any particular journal.

I compiled the list by searching through the editorial boards on journal websites. The selection criteria are somewhat arbitrary, so it is likely that there are mistakes and oversights. Please send corrections or updates to Dan Isaksen at isaksen.dan@gmail.com. Special thanks go to Bogdan Gheorghe and Gustavo Granja for their help in maintaining the list. This page was last updated in April 2025.
