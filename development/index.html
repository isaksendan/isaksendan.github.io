electronic Computational Homotopy Theory (eCHT)

The electronic Computational Homotopy Theory (eCHT) online research community sponsors a variety of activities related to research in pure mathematics, mostly in algebraic topology, homotopy theory, and related topics. The links in the sidebar lead to more detailed descriptions of our activities and information on how to participate.
